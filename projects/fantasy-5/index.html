FANTASY FOOTBALL · SEASON TWO CONCEPT

Fantasy 5.

A phone-first fantasy football concept shaped around small-sided games. Pick a five-player team, make changes across the gameweek, and follow a season through tables, match moments and stats.

Product concept · Demo data is fictional

THE IDEA

Fantasy football, scaled to five.

The prototype focuses on the rhythm of a compact fantasy season: set a team, manage substitutes and transfers, then return for league positions, highlights and personal stats. The interface is designed first for a phone, with a small-sided pitch at the centre of the experience.

01 / PICK

Build a five-player team

Choose a starting five and keep two substitutes ready to swap in.

02 / FOLLOW

Move through a season

Use gameweek controls, check league standings and revisit earlier rounds.

03 / REVIEW

See the story behind points

Explore match highlights and a personal stats area alongside your team.

PILOT SCORING

Points for the small details.

The concept tests a scoring model that rewards contributions beyond goals. These are prototype rules, not a live competition format.

+1Appearance
+5Goal
+3Assist
+5Each complete 10-minute clean-sheet period
+1Tackle
+1Block
−1Foul
0Shots and saves
